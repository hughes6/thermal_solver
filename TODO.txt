Yep — this screenshot finally makes the failure clear. The parser was still assuming the data belonging to a contact appears physically between 01 and 02, 02 and 03, etc. Your PDF is not doing that. It is interleaving whole column streams, so Output, Analog, descriptions, and the next signal can appear in an order that makes a row-based parser impossible to trust.

I rewrote the 5-column Contact Allocation parser around that. It now reconstructs each column independently, in order:

Contact Designation → 01, 02, 03...

Unit Signal Designation → RA0_THERM_P, RA0_SW_P, RA0_OSW_D_A, RA1_THERM_P, RA1_SW_P, RA1_OSW_D_A, RESERVED

I/F Direction → Output... Output, NA

Signal Type → Analog... Analog, NA

Then it zips those streams back into rows. That means Unit Signal can no longer absorb Output, Analog, a description, or the next Unit Signal, regardless of where Acrobat inserts \n.
